3. **AP angle**: a scheme, data point, or local example (both exams reward it)

## 6. The revision engine | పునశ్చరణ వ్యవస్థ

Most aspirants fail because they forget, not because they never studied. Use **1-3-7-21 spaced revision**:

| When | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Day +1 | Re-read today's short notes | 10 min |
| Day +3 | 10 self-made MCQs from that topic | 10 min |
| Day +7 | Saturday weekly test | in test slot |
| Day +21 | Revision loop: notes + error log | 15 min |

**Three notebooks only:**
1. **Short Notes** (1 page per topic, with the Telugu term next to the English term, e.g., *Fundamental Rights – ప్రాథమిక హక్కులు*)
2. **Error Log**: every wrong or guessed MCQ, with the reason and the correct concept. Re-read it every Saturday
3. **CA Notebook**: month-wise, tagged by subject (Polity / Eco / S&T / AP / Intl)

## 7. After G1 Prelims: Phases 4 & 5 | ప్రిలిమ్స్ తర్వాత

### Phase 4: G2 Screening + G1 Mains build (from 25 Jan, until G2 Screening)
- **Week 1 after the exam:** rest for 2 days. Check the answer key and estimate your score. Then start **Indian Society** (the only big G2-only block): social structure, family, caste, tribes, women, social movements, social issues, welfare mechanisms, and constitutional safeguards.
- **Daily split:** 55% G2 screening (Indian Society, re-revise History and Geography, Mental Ability, CA) and 45% G1 Mains (2 answers a day, 1 essay a week).
- **Last 3 weeks before G2 Screening:** G2 full mocks every other day (150 Qs in 150 min).

### Phase 5: Mains Double
**G1 Mains (descriptive, 750 marks).** This is the rank-decider.
- Write a **full timed paper every week** (rotate P-I to P-V), plus **2 answers a day**.
- Build each answer as: **intro → body (subheadings, data, AP example, diagram or map) → way forward**.
- Use **AP-specific data**: Socio-Economic Survey, Budget, Vision 2047, the Reorganisation Act, Polavaram, Amaravati, the coastal economy.
- **Telugu & English qualifying:** 1 practice set every week (letter, précis, translation, comprehension). These papers are easy, but **failing them cancels your attempt**.
- P-III Ethics and P-V Environment are G1-only. Give each 4–5 focused days.

**G2 Mains (objective, 300 marks).**
- Your G1 Mains reading already covers it. Switch to **fact-dense MCQ mode** for AP Social & Cultural History, Constitution, Economy (India + AP), and S&T.
- Target **3,000+ topic MCQs** and **8 full G2 Mains mocks**.
- **CPT:** practise MS Office and basic computer tasks for 30 min daily, 3 weeks before the test.

### Five rules that decide selection
1. **Fixed syllabus, one source per subject, revised 3 times** beats 10 books read once.
2. **Tests are learning, not judgement.** The analysis is worth more than the score.
3. **AP angle everywhere.** It is the difference between a generic answer and a selected one.
4. **Start writing early.** Groups are lost in G1 Mains, not in Prelims.
5. **Sleep, exercise, consistency.** 8 good hours every day for 100 days beats 14 hours for 20 days.
