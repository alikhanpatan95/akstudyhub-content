# AK Study Hub – Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 21 October 2026 · App: AK STUDY HUB (com.akstudyhub.app)_

AK Study Hub is a study app for APPSC Group 1 and Group 2 aspirants. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. In short: the app has no sign-up or login, and it does not collect or share your personal data.

## Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, location, contacts or photos. The app does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Information stored on your phone

Your language choice, test scores, bookmarks, study-plan progress and unlock codes are saved only on your own device, inside the app's storage. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

## Downloading study content

To show the latest current affairs, quizzes and revision sheets, the app downloads content files from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Like any website, GitHub receives standard technical information such as your IP address when the file is downloaded. Please see GitHub's privacy statement for how GitHub handles this. We do not receive this information.

## Payments

If you buy a premium plan, payment happens in your own UPI app (for example PhonePe, Google Pay or Paytm) or on the payment page you choose. The AK Study Hub app does not see or store your bank, card or UPI details. If you contact us on WhatsApp or Telegram about a payment, we see only what you send us there.

## Links to other apps

The app has links to YouTube, WhatsApp, Telegram and Google Drive. When you open them, those services' own privacy policies apply.

## Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for competitive exams. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
